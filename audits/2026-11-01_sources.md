## Model-related corrections

- SRC-0006 discusses heat stress, not primary empirical cycling mechanical-efficiency estimates; avoid citing it for generic 20–30% efficiency.
- SRC-0018 is specifically about cycling efficiency across age, sex, and training groups, not a standalone source supporting an arbitrary universal efficiency assumption.
- SRC-0011 primary study reports 26 serious adverse events among 19 PRIMA implant participants; any benefit-risk discussion must preserve this outcome.
- SRC-0013 evaluates wearable sensory cue integration; cannot directly justify permanently internal new sensory organs.
- SRC-0014 studies healthspan-related functional/QoL proxies, not mortality or maximum lifespan.

## Remaining evidence work

Read complete methodology and supplementary data, check corrections/retractions, extract sample sizes and confidence intervals in a machine-readable claims table, test applicability to healthy-human augmentation, and acquire independently replicable underlying datasets.

Legacy reported fields in sources.json and the complete literature_register.csv remain preserved; original reported_key_result fields are not erased.

## Integration verification and date clarifications

All 21 report rows were matched to the unchanged registry IDs. During integration,
19 publication identities and accessible abstracts were cross-checked using the
Europe PMC core API (DOI or PMCID query; PubMed locators are recorded per source).
SRC-0008 was checked on the FDA product page, SRC-0021 on the NIH indexed summary.
These are bibliography and abstract/scope checks only; no source received a
systematic full-text appraisal, dataset replication or empirical validation.
Targeted full-text passages were inspected for SRC-0001 (weighted median/IQR),
SRC-0003 (integration limitations and correction notice), SRC-0004 (diffusion
context), and SRC-0013 (wearable framework); this does not constitute full-text
quality assessment. Other sources remain at abstract or official summary level.

The single year in the proposal denotes online publication for SRC-0011
(2025-10-20; 2026 issue), SRC-0012 (2025-10-12; 2026 issue), SRC-0013
(2024-10-28; indexed year 2025), and SRC-0016 (2025-11-28; 2026 issue).
This distinction corrects ambiguity without overwriting imported dates.
SRC-0003 contains a figure 5 replacement notice dated 2026-08-17: the proposal's
blanket absence of a correction check must not hide this observed correction.
No comprehensive correction/retraction clearance is claimed for any source.

The claim/source warnings for SRC-0006 and SRC-0018 concern applicability to the
project's universal efficiency assumption. Abstract-level review alone does not
prove those papers never discuss efficiency or workload elsewhere in full text.
Original Foundation report wording (including its claim of checked publications)
is historical, not the current evidence classification; this audit and registry
annotations supersede its interpretation while all original bytes are retained.
