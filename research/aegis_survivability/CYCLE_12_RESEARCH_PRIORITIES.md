## 2. Priority 1: Prospective Keratin Coupon Acquisition (Hoof & Pangolin)

### 2.1 The High-Cystine Hoof Wall Challenge
- **Objective:** Validate the Cycle 11 9-parameter physics-informed model on independent equine hoof wall coupons across the complete hydration continuum ($0\%\text{ to }100\%\text{ RH}$).
- **Target Primary Sources:**
  - Kasapi M.A. & Gosline J.M. (1997). *Design complexity and fracture toughness in the equine hoof wall*. J. Exp. Biol. 200: 1639–1659.
  - Kitchener A. (1987). *Fracture toughness of horn sheath and hoof wall*. J. Zool. 213: 621–638.
- **Success Criterion:** Model must predict the $14.6\text{ GPa}$ dry modulus and the non-linear hydration softening curve within $\text{MAE} < 1.5\text{ GPa}$ ($>65\%$ error reduction over horn baseline) using measured cystine ($[SS] \approx 14.5\text{ mol}\%$) and tubule porosity ($p \approx 0.015$) with **zero parameter re-tuning**.

### 2.2 Scale Armor Coupon Cross-Validation
- **Objective:** Acquire micro-tensile and 3-point flexural data from pangolin (*Manis javanica*) or armadillo osteoderm scutes to test whether the laminated planar scale formulation holds across non-bovid species.
- **Target Primary Sources:**
  - Wang B. et al. (2016). *Pangolin armor: Microstructure and mechanical properties*. J. Mech. Behav. Biomed. Mater. 55: 92–102.
  - Chen P.-Y. et al. (2011). *Structure and mechanical properties of selected protective biomaterials*. J. Mech. Behav. Biomed. Mater. 4(5): 687–695.

## 4. Priority 3: Cellular & Morphogenetic Placode Induction in Mammalian Skin

### 4.1 Developmental Biology of Epidermal Scale Patterning
- **Objective:** Model the gene regulatory network (GRN) governing epidermal placode condensation in mammalian skin.
- **Specific Cellular Pathways:**
  - Wnt/$\beta$-catenin activation versus Dkk4 inhibition (Turing reaction-diffusion wave for scale spacing).
  - EDA/EDAR signaling: determining whether the placode develops into a cylindrical hair follicle or a flattened macroscopic scale plate.
  - Keratin-associated protein (KAP) expression profiles: analyzing how to induce high-sulfur matrix expression without premature cellular apoptosis.
- **Hypothesis to Test:** Pulsed modulation of BMP4/Wnt3a in eutherian skin organoids can induce localized hyper-keratinization with oriented intermediate filament bundles resembling ancestral scale matrices.

### 5.1 Real-World Validation of the 18% Open-Skin Boundary
- **Objective:** Compare the lumped 1-node thermodynamic model against published human exercise hyperthermia trials involving impermeable encapsulated body gear.
- **Target Sources:**
  - Gagnon D. & Crandall C.G. (2018). *Sweating as a heat loss thermoeffector*. Handb. Clin. Neurol. 156: 211–232.
  - Sawka M.N. et al. (2007). *Human water needs for physical exertion and environment*. ACSM Position Stand.
- **Calibration Goal:** Validate the effective armor vapor resistance $R_{e,\text{armor}} = 0.045\text{ kPa}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{W}$ and verify whether the $15\text{--}18\%$ open skin window prevents heat accumulation in living human subjects running at $3.5\text{ m/s}$ in $35^\circ\text{C}$ / $50\%\text{ RH}$.
