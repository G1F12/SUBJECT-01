## 4. Why ARCH-BIO Must Not Be Discarded

While Cycle 4B highlighted the severe thermoregulatory penalty of ARCH-BIO in tropical environments ($40\%$ eccrine gland occlusion capping evaporative cooling at $340\text{ W}$), ARCH-BIO possesses fundamental biological advantages over biohybrids:
1. **Zero Foreign Body Pathology:** Fully endogenous tissues express self-antigens and possess full living microvascular perfusion. They do not trigger chronic foreign body giant-cell reactions, aseptic osteolysis, or sterile seromas.
2. **Continuous Self-Repair:** Living bone and keratinized dermis continuously remodel via osteoclasts/osteoblasts and basal keratinocytes, repairing sub-critical microcracks before fatigue failure can occur. In contrast, titanium implants accumulate cyclic fatigue damage indefinitely without biological renewal.
3. **Infection Clearance:** Living tissues mount active humoral and cellular immune responses (neutrophil and macrophage extravasation). A biohybrid titanium lattice that becomes colonized by *Staphylococcus aureus* forms an antibiotic-resistant biofilm that requires surgical excision.
4. **Resolution:** ARCH-BIO research must continue in parallel, focusing on solving the thermoregulatory occlusion problem via fenestrated scale patterning and active vascular cooling shunts.
