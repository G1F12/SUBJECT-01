## 1. Epistemic Demarcation: Prediction vs. Enhancement

> [!CAUTION]
> **Core Scientific Principle of SUBJECT-01:**
> Demonstrating that a regression model can predict human knee torque with $R^2 = 0.6867$ using body mass, height, biological sex, and joint angle **does NOT equate to biological human enhancement**.
> High predictive accuracy reflects anatomical scaling laws (PCSA $\propto$ mass/height; torque $\propto$ PCSA $\cdot$ moment arm). Conflating model accuracy with actual physical augmentation is strictly prohibited.

## 3. Physiological Limits Identified in Cycle 17

### 3.1. The Antagonist Co-Activation Safety Limit
Experiment E1 proved that healthy human adults maintain a baseline of ~8–11% antagonist hamstrings co-activation during maximal isometric knee extension.
- **Biomechanical Function:** Co-activation provides joint compression, equalizes articular contact pressures, and restrains anterior tibial translation, directly protecting the anterior cruciate ligament (ACL).
- **Enhancement Limit:** Any theoretical intervention aimed at "zeroing out" antagonist co-activation to maximize net extension torque would drastically elevate shear forces on the cruciate ligaments, causing immediate articular joint failure.

### 3.2. Intra-Tendinous Shearing Limits
Experiment E2 confirmed that the deep Achilles tendon layer displaces **$23.1\%$ ($0.564\text{ mm}$) more** than the superficial layer during isometric plantarflexion ($p = 0.0031$).
- **Tissue Vulnerability:** This displacement mismatch is accommodated by the non-collagenous interfascicular matrix (IFM), rich in lubricin, decorin, and COMP.
- **Enhancement Limit:** If muscular force is augmented rapidly without allowing the IFM to remodel and adapt its lubricated sliding surfaces, localized shear stress concentrations will induce delamination micro-tears and tendinopathy.

## 4. Reconciled View of Tendon Plasticity

The synthesis of Cycles 16 and 17 provides a balanced, realistic understanding of tendon mechanics:
1. **The Extreme Negative Claim is Refuted:** The claim that a $+20\%$ muscle strength gain automatically destroys tendons via a $-75\%$ fatigue lifespan collapse was an artifact of applying high-stress Basquin power laws to unadapted cadaveric specimens under constant maximal loads. In real human life, daily task loads are submaximal and dictated by body mass.
2. **The Extreme Positive Claim is Refuted:** Tendons cannot rapidly increase cross-sectional area overnight. While core collagen turnover is negligible in adults ($^{14}\text{C}$ radiocarbon limit), functional adaptation occurs over 12–16 weeks via enzymatic lysyl oxidase (LOX) cross-linking, increased Young's modulus, and IFM remodeling.
