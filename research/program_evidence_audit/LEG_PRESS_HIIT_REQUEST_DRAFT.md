Subject: Data request — cycling work, isometric leg press and jump outcomes

Dear authors,

Your study “Effects of Different Recovery Periods Following a Very Intense Interval Training Session on Strength and Explosive Performance in Elite Female Ice Hockey Players” (doi:10.1519/JSC.0000000000004782) reports cycling external work and repeated measures of isometric leg press and countermovement jumps following different recovery periods. We are assessing computational models of force, power and recovery using existing data; we do not perform biological interventions.

If permitted, could you share de-identified participant-linked data for the rested, 10-minute, 6-hour and 24-hour conditions: individualized wVO2max and body mass; per-session and per-bout measured cycling power, cadence, durations and total work; time from exercise cessation; individual leg-press force and RFD for all trials and sets, jump force, velocity, power, height and joint angles; trial inclusion/exclusion, missingness, data dictionary and processing code? Please state if work was measured per bout and whether sessions were matched on actual work or only on prescribed settings. We would also appreciate clarification of participant overlap with your 2023 leg-press test-retest study (doi:10.3390/sports11050096), any correction/version, and terms of reuse.

We will avoid interpreting the existing data as a 45-minute or human-enhancement trial and will not request identifiers.

Thank you for your consideration.
