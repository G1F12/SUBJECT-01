Subject: Clarification of MVC trial aggregation in Dataset S1, DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0112090

Dear authors,

We are attempting a reproducible secondary analysis of the individual MVC recovery data in Dataset S1 of your 2014 article. The Methods describe saving the best of three MVC attempts, and the supplementary dictionary identifies Bp11, Bp12 and Bp13 as successive measurements. In the 17 participants with at least two complete sessions, converting Kp to newtons and taking the maximum of the three fields produces a higher group mean than Table 1, whereas the mean of the three fields approximately reproduces it.

Could you clarify the exact mapping of these fields, whether any averaging or other preprocessing was applied before Table 1, the conversion and rounding, and which observations or missing-value rules were used per condition? If available, a pseudonymous analysis-ready dataset or original processing script with definitions and reuse conditions would be helpful. We are not assuming an error; a description of the procedure would allow us to choose the correct primary outcome.

Thank you.
