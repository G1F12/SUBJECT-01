Subject: Availability of individual leg-press force and motion data

Dear authors,

We are assessing whether an independently measured leg-press dataset can test a biomechanical model. Would the individual trial-level data underlying your 2017 paper (DOI: 10.1080/13873954.2017.1336633) be available for research use, and under what license or conditions?

The useful minimum would be pseudonymous participant/trial IDs and synchronised calibrated force vectors or measurement axis, sled position, hip/knee/ankle marker trajectories, segment lengths, task conditions and data dictionary, including sampling rates, filtering, coordinate conventions and force-plate orientation. If measured, trunk/seat orientation, foot positions, participant body mass and calibration information would help assess individual geometry. We understand that some variables may not have been measured or retained; please indicate available variables and any restrictions.

Thank you.
