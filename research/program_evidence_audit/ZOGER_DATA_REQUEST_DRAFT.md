**Subject:** Request for anonymized individual data for independent leg-press model evaluation

Dear corresponding author,

I am conducting a computational research project on the transfer of muscle force to a bilateral isometric leg press. Your 2025 study reports repeated measurements at internal knee angles of 100° and 140°. Its individual observations could support an independent comparison between simple anthropometric predictors and a closed-chain model.

Would you be willing to share a de-identified, participant-level dataset for the bilateral isometric conditions? The most useful fields would be a pseudonymous participant ID, session and trial IDs, side and total calibrated peak force in newtons, target and measured joint angles, age, sex, body mass and height, relevant segment lengths, foot position and footplate geometry, and definitions of the recorded force axis and units. Repeated trials and timing or filtering details would allow assessment of within-person variability. If center-of-pressure or force-vector components were recorded, those would be useful for assessing the contact constraints.

A data dictionary, protocol version, information on excluded trials, license or data-use conditions, and any required citation would be appreciated. We can work with a narrower dataset and report explicitly which model comparisons it supports. No identifying records are needed.

Thank you for considering the request.
