Subject: Clarification of subject linkage and mechanical variables in Zenodo record 19947401

Dear authors,

We are assessing whether the v2 dataset (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.19947401) can support a reproducible, participant-held-out analysis of post-exercise recovery. Could you clarify the following points or provide a pseudonymous crosswalk and processing documentation under an appropriate license?

1. The README describes 32 athletes in a two-condition crossover. The four Raw_Strength sheets contain 48 distinct Subject_ID strings and each string appears under one Group_Clean condition. How should the identifiers in the worksheets and Participants_Metadata_Judo.csv be linked across conditions, loads and sessions, and what is the correct participant count?
2. Fields described as repetitions to failure frequently contain non-integers at all time points. Are these averages, transformed estimates, imputed values or another measure? Please provide original trial-level counts and definitions where available, together with timestamps and missing-data processing.
3. The codebook labels TW as mean barbell velocity in m/s, while spreadsheet values span about 7.6–30.6. Please confirm the unit, scaling, filter and calibration and provide representative raw calibrated movement data and calculations. Please also document the origin and unit of peak power and the force/load and duration needed to compute actual work.
4. Please provide the randomized condition order, testing and recovery timing, exercise load and per-participant mass at each relevant visit, with any limits on reuse. If variables were not measured or are unavailable, that information is also helpful.

Thank you for considering this request.
