Subject: Individual torque and power time series for DOI 10.1113/EP090444

Dear authors,

We are evaluating reproducible, participant-held-out predictions of force and work during cycling. Would the pseudonymous individual data underlying your 2023 study, DOI 10.1113/EP090444, be available for secondary research under stated reuse conditions?

The requested fields are participant and visit IDs with the mapping across HVY and VHI, body mass and anthropometry, condition order, timestamps and durations; continuous measured external power, cadence and torque, calibration and units; the start and end times of each 3-second maximal isokinetic effort, bilateral crank torque and power summaries or signals, the exact peak computation and exclusions; and any pre-, immediate post- and recovery force measurements and temperature or oxygen uptake channels, where actually recorded. A data dictionary, missingness rules and analysis code would allow us to compare measured mechanical work and force without assuming that target power was delivered.

We understand some fields or post-exercise measures may not exist; please identify what was measured and any restrictions. Thank you for considering the request.
